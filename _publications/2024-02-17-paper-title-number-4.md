excerpt: 'This paper introduces SHARE, a new long-term open-domain dialogue dataset constructed from 1,201 movie scripts that includes persona profiles, personal events, mutual events, and implicitly inferred shared memories between speakers 
. It proposes EPISODE, a framework that dynamically selects, manages, and updates these memory types during response generation to support coherent and engaging multi-session conversations 
. To maintain concise and relevant context over time, the authors develop memory-management strategies—accumulation, logical updates for sequential or conflicting events, and deduplication—to efficiently handle growing memory sets 
. Experiments using automatic metrics (BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore) and GPT-4–assisted evaluations demonstrate that leveraging shared memories significantly improves coherence, engagingness, and reflectiveness compared to baselines . Both the SHARE dataset and EPISODE codebase are publicly released on GitHub to facilitate further research in memory-aware long-term dialogue systems 
.'
