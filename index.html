Long-Term Conversational Recommendation

EMNLP 2026 Main Conference
Nov 2025 — PresentKU-KT AI CenterProject lead · Equal-contribution author

Personalized recommendation depends on remembering how a user’s preferences change. This project brings long-term conversational recommendation and memory-updating agents together to maintain behavioral and dialogue personas across sessions.

My contributions

  • Led the end-to-end design and implementation of the long-term CRS project.
  • Built a framework that outperformed established multi-session recommendation baselines.
  • Developed a memory-updating agent to maintain behavioral and dialogue personas across sessions.
  • Designed quantitative diversity evaluation, demonstrating higher dataset diversity than prior CRS benchmarks.

ETRI · SOLAR

Personalized Sleep Prediction from Lifelog Data

Grand Prize · 대상
2026Korea University · Team 멀왕5th ETRI Human Understanding AI Paper ChallengeICTC 2026 · Accepted

SOLAR predicts sleep, fatigue, and stress by separating a person’s long-term baseline from day-to-day changes. The framework anchors predictions in accumulated personal history, then refines them with daily sensor evidence and temporal context.

Grand Prize · Minister of Science and ICT Award

과학기술정보통신부장관상 · Team 멀왕: Kyubin Kim, Chanho Park, Kyuho Lee, and Euntae Kim.

Research approach

  • Combine long-term personal patterns with recent lifelog and sensor observations.
  • Predict seven sleep-, fatigue-, and stress-related indicators with a stepwise anchor-and-refine framework.
  • Analyze which signals influence predictions and validate the reproducibility of the research.

Focus

Research Interests

I study natural language processing and multimodal AI to build personalized conversational agents that remember, understand, and adapt to users over long-term interactions.

01

Long-term & Memory-aware Dialogue

Dialogue systems that extract, retrieve, and update user memories to maintain coherent interactions across sessions.

02

Conversational Recommendation

Persona-aware recommender systems that track evolving preferences and behavioral personas over time.

03

Agentic Memory Management

Memory-updating agents that decide what to retain, revise, retrieve, and forget for personalized interaction.

04

Multimodal Personalization

Conversational agents that combine language and visual context for adaptive, human-centered experiences.
